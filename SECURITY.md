## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in the Stock Charting Library, we strongly encourage you to disclose it **responsibly**.

- Please **do not** publicly disclose the issue before we have had a chance to address it.
- Instead, report it privately to us via **[INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]** 📧

When reporting a vulnerability, please include as much detail as possible:

- A clear description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Any suggested fixes (optional but appreciated)

## Our Commitment

We will:

- Respond to your report within **5 business days**
- Provide an initial assessment of the issue
- Keep you updated as we investigate and mitigate the vulnerability
- Credit you (if desired) in release notes or acknowledgments

## Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

- Avoid exploiting the vulnerability beyond the minimum necessary to demonstrate it.
- Do not access, modify, or delete data without explicit permission.
- Do not publicly disclose or share details of the vulnerability before a fix is released.
